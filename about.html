Our Story

From a Small Studio to a Global Creative Force

Avento was born in a tiny Brooklyn loft in 2012. What started as two designers obsessing over typography and whitespace has grown into a 28-person studio that has shaped the visual identities of brands across five continents.

We have always believed that design is not decoration — it is the bridge between a business and its people. Every pixel, every word, every interaction is an opportunity to build trust, communicate value, and create genuine human connection.

Today, Avento operates at the intersection of strategy, design, and technology. We partner with startups, scale-ups, and established enterprises who share our conviction that remarkable work is worth the effort.

What We Believe

Our Core Values

These are the principles that guide every decision we make and every project we take on.

Bold Creativity

We push the boundaries of what's expected. Average is the enemy of remarkable, and we relentlessly pursue work that surprises, delights, and inspires.

Radical Honesty

We tell our clients the truth, even when it's uncomfortable. Transparent communication builds stronger partnerships and better outcomes for everyone.

Purpose-Driven Work

Every design decision, every line of code is driven by a clear purpose. We design with intent — always asking "why" before deciding "what."

Continuous Learning

The industry never stops evolving and neither do we. Our team dedicates time every week to learning new tools, techniques, and perspectives.

True Partnership

We don't work for our clients — we work with them. The best results emerge from genuine collaboration and mutual trust built over time.

Lasting Impact

We build for the long term. Our work is crafted to stand the test of time — in quality, in relevance, and in the real impact it makes for your business.

Our Journey

A Decade of Growth & Milestones

From humble beginnings to industry recognition — the key moments that have shaped who we are today.

2012

Studio Founded

Marcus Rivera and Sophia Chen launch Avento from a Brooklyn loft with a laptop, a passion for design, and zero clients.

2013

First Major Client

Landed a full brand redesign for a $50M Series A fintech startup, putting Avento on the map in the New York creative scene.

2015

Team Expands to 10

Grew to 10 full-time creatives. Opened our first proper studio space in Dumbo, Brooklyn with room to breathe and think big.

2017

First International Project

Partnered with a luxury retail brand in London, our first international engagement and the start of our global client roster.

2019

Awwwards Recognition

Won three Awwwards Site of the Day honors in a single year, cementing our reputation as a world-class digital design studio.

2021

100th Project Milestone

Celebrated completing our 100th project — a custom e-commerce platform that generated $2M in sales in its first month live.

2023

Agency of the Year

Named "Digital Agency of the Year" by Communication Arts — the proudest moment in our studio's history to date.

2026

Today & Beyond

28 team members, 320+ projects, 14 countries — and we're just getting started. The best work of our lives is still ahead.
