Got Questions?

Everything You Need to Know

Can't find your answer? Reach out to us directly and we'll get back to you within one business day.

What kind of clients do you work with?

We work with a wide range of clients — from early-stage startups and scale-ups to established enterprises and global brands. What matters most to us is not the size of the company but the quality of the challenge and the alignment in values. We love working with founders and teams who are ambitious, collaborative, and believe that great design is a strategic asset.

Our clients span industries including fintech, health & wellness, retail, hospitality, technology, professional services, and consumer goods.

How long has Enfold been in business?

Enfold was founded in 2012 in Brooklyn, New York. Over the past 14 years, we have grown from a two-person design studio into a 28-person full-service creative agency. We have completed more than 320 projects for clients in 14 countries and won 46 Pre-Built Layouts along the way.

Do you work with clients outside the United States?

Absolutely. We have worked with clients across North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, and the Middle East. Our team is fully distributed and experienced in working across time zones. We use async-first communication tools and schedule regular video calls to ensure seamless collaboration regardless of where you are in the world.

How much does a typical project cost?

Project investment varies depending on scope, complexity, and timeline. As a general guide:

  • Brand Identity: $4,000 – $18,000
  • Marketing Website: $8,000 – $35,000
  • E-Commerce Platform: $12,000 – $60,000
  • Mobile App: $25,000 – $120,000
  • Full Brand + Website: $15,000 – $50,000

Every project starts with a free discovery call where we scope your requirements accurately and provide a fixed-price proposal. We never surprise you with hidden costs.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. Standard project payments are structured as: 40% at project kickoff, 40% at the midpoint design approval stage, and 20% on final delivery. For larger projects (over $20,000), we can discuss quarterly payment structures. We accept bank transfer, credit card, and Wise for international clients.

Are there ongoing costs after project delivery?

The project fee covers design, development, and delivery. After launch, you may choose to invest in hosting (which we can arrange), ongoing maintenance retainers, or marketing services — but none of these are mandatory. We offer three support tiers: a basic plan ($299/month), a growth plan ($799/month), and a priority plan ($1,499/month). Details are outlined in your project proposal.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines depend on scope and complexity. Here are typical ranges:

  • Brand Identity: 4 – 8 weeks
  • Marketing Website (5–10 pages): 6 – 10 weeks
  • Complex Website (15+ pages): 10 – 16 weeks
  • Mobile App: 16 – 26 weeks
  • E-Commerce Platform: 10 – 20 weeks

We always define a clear timeline in the proposal and use project management tools (Notion + Linear) to keep everything on track and visible to you throughout.

How involved will I need to be during the project?

We designed our process to be efficient with your time while keeping you genuinely informed. Expect structured touchpoints at key milestones — typically a kickoff session, a design review at 30%, a major checkpoint at 60%, and a pre-launch review. Outside of these, we work asynchronously and consolidate feedback into clear, time-boxed rounds. Most clients invest 2–3 hours per week during active phases.

How many rounds of revisions are included?

Our standard projects include two structured revision rounds per major deliverable. In practice, by the time we reach a formal revision round, we are already well-aligned thanks to our ongoing communication — so most projects are approved without needing all available revisions. Revisions beyond the included rounds are billed at our hourly rate, which is disclosed upfront in your contract.

What happens after the project is launched?

Every project includes a 30-day post-launch support window at no extra charge. During this period, we address any bugs, browser compatibility issues, or minor adjustments that arise. After that, you can either manage independently (we provide full documentation and training), or move to one of our support retainer plans for ongoing care, updates, and growth services.

Do you provide training on how to use our website or app?

Yes, always. Every project delivery includes a live training session (recorded for your reference), detailed written documentation, and video walkthroughs of your CMS or admin panel. We want you to feel completely confident managing your own digital presence — and we are always available for follow-up questions.

What technologies do you build with?

Our technology choices are always driven by your project's specific requirements, not personal preference. We are fluent across a wide stack including React, Next.js, Vue.js, Node.js, Python/Django, WordPress, Shopify, Sanity, Contentful, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, AWS, Vercel, and more. For mobile, we work with Swift (iOS), Kotlin (Android), React Native, and Flutter. We will always recommend the right tool for your job.

Will my website be optimized for search engines?

Yes. All websites we build include foundational technical SEO as standard: clean semantic HTML, proper heading hierarchy, Open Graph meta tags, schema markup, XML sitemap, robots.txt configuration, performance optimization, and Core Web Vitals alignment. For clients who want to pursue aggressive SEO growth, we offer dedicated SEO strategy and content services as an add-on or retainer.

Who owns the source code and design files?

You do. Upon final payment, all intellectual property — source code, design files (Figma), assets, and documentation — transfers fully to you. We retain the right to showcase the work in our portfolio unless you request otherwise via a mutual NDA. We believe your investment should result in full ownership, full stop.
